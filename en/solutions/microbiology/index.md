# Microbiology

Documented traceability from sterilization to incubation

- Sterilization: Autoclave: cycle, F0 and air check
- Aseptic work: Hood: airflow and filter status during the session
- Incubation: Incubator: continuous dual-sensor temperature
- Batch record: One QR code links the three stages in a single report

ISO 7218, ISO 11133, NOM-059-SSA1, ISO/IEC 17025
