# Industrias Figursa

Autoclaves, hoods, climate chambers and incubators made in Mexico and connected to FIGURSA Cloud: tamper-evident records, alarms and audit-ready reports, serviced by the people who build them.

## Solutions

- Microbiology: Documented traceability from sterilization to incubation
- Universities: Operational continuity for teaching and research
- Stability: Controlled conditions and tamper-evident records for stability studies
- Food analysis: Protein and fat determination with traceable results
