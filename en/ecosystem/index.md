# Ecosystem

- Signed at the device: Every reading is signed inside the unit by a security chip before it leaves.
- Chained and verifiable: Each record carries the fingerprint of the previous one; any alteration shows.
- Sealed with legal validity: A daily NOM-151 conservation certificate fixes the record in time.
- Your data, always exportable: PDF, CSV and an open API; LIMS integration.
- Early detection: Physical and statistical models detect compressor, heater and gasket wear before a failure.
- Every cycle verified: In autoclaves, the air check and F0 lethality are computed for every cycle.
- Assisted reports: The system drafts reports and alarm explanations; a person reviews and signs them. The original record is never modified.
