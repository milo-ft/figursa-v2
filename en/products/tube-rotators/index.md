# Tube rotators

A rotator that disperses the sample evenly and mixes several components at once, with constant, gentle movement in every plane. It takes tubes of different sizes and diameters.

## Features

- 18 rpm speed
- 28 cm disc with 12 holders
- Base approx. 15 × 21 cm
- 127 V, 60 Hz power
