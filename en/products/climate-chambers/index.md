# Climate chambers

Microprocessor-controlled temperature and humidity chambers for food studies, accelerated ageing, genetics, viral research, environmental science, biochemistry and growth studies. From 72 to 976 litres, with refrigerated versions and special sizes.

## Features

- Digital temperature control: ambient + 5 °C to 70 °C, ± 0.1 °C sensitivity
- Digital humidity control: 10 to 90 % RH
- T-304 stainless interior, magnetic seal
- Optional temperature and humidity recorder
- Optional computer interface and supervisory systems
- 120 V, 60 Hz power

## Frequently asked questions

**Do you build refrigerated chambers?**
Yes. Besides the CH series we build refrigerated and growth chambers such as the Germinador 388 (−5 to 55 °C). Tell us the range your protocol requires.

**What installation do they need?**
A distilled-water supply, a condensate drain and a 120 V, 60 Hz outlet.
