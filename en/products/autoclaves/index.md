# Autoclaves

Vertical steam autoclaves for sterilizing labware, culture media, instruments and waste. Stainless-steel tank, cabinet and basket, a cast chrome-plated bronze lid, and three control levels: manual, automatic, and digital with timer.

## Features

- Working pressure up to 30 PSI
- Temperature up to 135 °C
- Safety valve and relief valve
- Dial pressure gauge and temperature indicator
- External water-level gauge
- Stainless-steel tank, cabinet and basket
- Cast, chrome-plated bronze lid

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference between the AV, AVA and AVAD models?**
The AV series has manual control with three heat settings; the AVA regulates the cycle automatically; the AVAD adds digital control and a timer. All three share stainless-steel construction, 30 PSI and 135 °C.

**Which autoclave size do I need?**
The 25 × 50 cm chambers cover the daily use of a teaching laboratory; 30 × 60 and 35 × 60 cm suit culture media in volume and waste. Our team can help you size it.
