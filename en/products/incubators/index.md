# Incubators

Incubators with solid-state electronic control and ± 0.1 °C sensitivity, T-304 stainless interior, an inner acrylic door with airtight seal and fibreglass insulation.

## Features

- Electronic control, ± 0.1 °C sensitivity
- Digital temperature display
- Inner acrylic door with airtight seal
- Magnetic seal
- 120 V, 60 Hz power
