# Fume hoods

Hoods for work with toxic, corrosive or irritating fumes. T-304 stainless interior, zinc-coated steel exterior with epoxy finish, and a counterweighted sash that stays at any height.

## Features

- Interior lighting and extractor
- Water and gas taps, electrical outlets (CEG series)
- Sash with shatter-proof glass or acrylic
- Optional explosion-proof and remote extractors
