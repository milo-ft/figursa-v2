# Dry-block tube incubators

Dry-block tube incubator with solid-state temperature control and a thermistor sensor, from ambient to 130 °C. Interchangeable blocks for 6 to 25 mm tubes.

## Features

- Range from ambient to 130 °C
- Solid-state control with thermistor sensor
- Blocks for 6, 10, 13, 16, 19 and 25 mm tubes, and a combined block
