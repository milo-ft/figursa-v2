# Heating mantles

Mantles with built-in temperature control, pilot light and front switch. Fibreglass body up to 500 mL and epoxy-finished zinc-coated steel from 500 mL to 22 L.

## Features

- Built-in temperature control
- 120 V, 60 Hz power
