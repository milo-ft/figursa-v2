# Water baths

Constant-temperature baths in T-304 stainless steel, from ambient to 60 °C without lid and up to 100 °C with lid, an electronic thermostat with 0.05 °C sensitivity and ± 0.1 °C accuracy. Digital control on every model.

## Features

- 0.05 °C sensitivity, ± 0.1 °C accuracy
- Low-density heater for long life
- Fibreglass insulation
