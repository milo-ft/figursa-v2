# Goldfisch fat extractors

Six-place Goldfisch-type solvent fat extractor for fast determination of fat and oil content, with solvents boiling at up to 85 °C. It complements Kjeldahl analysis in food science and food quality control.

## Features

- 6 condensers with holders for the thimble tube or recovery tube
- Individual 100 W heaters
- Time-proportioning thermostat for every two heaters
- Cooling water, 1/2" NPT connection
- 127 V, 60 Hz power
