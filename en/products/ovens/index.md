# Drying ovens

Ovens with solid-state electronic control from ambient up to 200, 220, 250 or 300 °C, ± 0.1 °C sensitivity, insulated double wall and two adjustable shelves. Forced-air and vacuum options.

## Features

- Up to 300 °C depending on model
- ± 0.1 °C sensitivity
- NiCr heater with ceramic insulators
- Top vent for vapours
