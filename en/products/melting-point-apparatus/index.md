# Melting-point apparatus

Apparatus to determine the melting point of one or more solids: capillary models with a platinum sensor, electronic control and digital display, and a temperature-controlled hot-stage model.

## Features

- Platinum temperature sensor
- Electronic control with heating-cycle control
- Digital display, 0.1 °C resolution
- 8X magnifier
- Aluminium block for 1 to 3 capillaries, ceramic-insulated
- Includes 100 capillary tubes
- Zinc-coated steel cabinet with epoxy finish
- 127 V, 60 Hz; 50 W maximum

## Applications

- Identification and purity of solids
- Pharmaceutical and chemical quality control
- Organic chemistry teaching
