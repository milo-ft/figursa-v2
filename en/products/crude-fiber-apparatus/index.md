# Crude fiber apparatus

A fast, safe and accurate condensate-reflux apparatus for crude fiber determination. The sample undergoes a simulated digestion: heated in acid, washed and heated in alkali to isolate cellulose and crude-fiber residue. A procedure listed by AOAC.

## Features

- 6 heaters with individual infinite-type temperature control
- Stainless-steel condensers
- 600 mL tall-form beakers
- Requires cooling water and drain
- 127 V, 60 Hz power
