# Colony counters

Counters to view and count bacterial colonies on glass or disposable Petri dishes, with a magnifier, a grid on glass, a dark contrast background and indirect reflected light across the whole area without tiring glare.

## Features

- Adjustable 3X magnifier, 100 mm diameter
- Fire-enamelled glass grid
- Dark contrast background and indirect reflected light
- Reset button and manual count button
- 127 V, 60 Hz power
