## Frequently asked questions

**Horizontal or vertical flow?**
Horizontal flow protects the product and is common for media preparation and assembly; vertical flow reduces air directed at the operator and suits admixtures and inoculation.

**How often is the HEPA filter replaced?**
It depends on use and environment; we recommend checking airflow velocity and filter integrity at least once a year. FIGURSA provides the service and replacement filters.
