# Pensky-Martens closed cup

Closed cup for flash-point determination of petroleum products and fuel oils. A smooth one-hand ignition mechanism, with cup, lid, manual stirrer, shutter and test-flame system.

## Features

- Meets ASTM D 93 and FSM 1102
- Low-density Ni-Cr heater and electronic control
- Stainless-steel cabinet; cup turned in bronze
- Chrome burner with 1/8" IPS connection
- Approx. 28 × 18 × 46 cm
- 127 V, 60 Hz; 750 W
