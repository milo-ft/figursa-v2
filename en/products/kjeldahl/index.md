# Kjeldahl apparatus

Digesters, distillers and combined units for protein and nitrogen determination by the Kjeldahl method, from 1 to 12 places, with reversible ceramic heaters for 500 or 800 mL flasks.

## Features

- Independent heating control per place
- T-304 stainless supports on an acid-resistant base
- Stainless distiller tubes welded to the condenser
- Motorized fume extraction on 6-place digesters
- Dial thermometer on the cooling-water outlet (6-place distillers)
