# Soxhlet extractors

Six-unit Vari-Heat Soxhlet extractor for extractions, distillations and digestions. Each unit has its own temperature control and pilot light, with round aluminium heaters and two supports per unit.

## Features

- Independent temperature control and pilot light per unit
- Round aluminium heaters
- Two supports per unit
- 127 V, 60 Hz power
