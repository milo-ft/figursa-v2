# FIGURSA Cloud

## Essential (Included with every FIGURSA unit)
- Real-time monitoring from the portal and the app
- Alarms by e-mail, SMS and notification
- 90-day history
- PDF and CSV export
## Compliance (Per unit, yearly)
- Everything in Essential, plus:
- Tamper-evident record with a daily NOM-151 seal
- Electronic signatures and audit trail
- Batch records and study manager
- Unlimited history
## Service (Per unit, yearly)
- Everything in Compliance, plus:
- Predictive maintenance
- Annual on-site verification; accredited calibration on request
- Priority response and backup unit
