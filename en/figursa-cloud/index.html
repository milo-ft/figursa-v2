Frequently asked questions

Does FIGURSA Cloud comply with 21 CFR Part 11 and NOM-059?

It is designed to support compliance: audit trail, electronic signatures, access control and tamper-evident records. Validating the system in your laboratory is the user's responsibility; FIGURSA provides the qualification documentation.

What happens if the connection drops?

The unit stores readings and sends them on reconnection; the record keeps its continuity.

Can I monitor other brands?

Yes, with FIGURSA Link, which measures temperature, humidity and door opening.
